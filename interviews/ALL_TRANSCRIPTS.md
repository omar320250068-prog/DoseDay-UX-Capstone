**INT:** Would you want the app to explain that?

**P1:** I would want it to say "this is normal, it settles, here's when to call." I would want it written by a person, not a robot. A robot telling me about my body is not something I trust.

**INT:** Refills.

**P1:** That's the thing that sneaks up on me. I'm not a hoarder, I don't want a month of boxes in the house. **INT:** Anything about your box — the physical one?

**INT:** Can you estimate — without it being a number you have to defend — how many doses you'd say you miss?

**P1:** It depends which week. If I'm on days off it's rare. If I've just come off nights, I would say one or two. Maybe. It's not something I count. My daughter would say I miss more than I say I miss.

**INT:** Last question. How old are you? The form said 58.

## Transcription convention

Bracketed text in a participant's turn is an observer note, written in the third person.
Everything outside the brackets is the participant's own speech, in the first person.
This convention was added on 2026-09-26: an earlier version of the demonstration stage
direction was written so that it read like a third party performing the routine, and the
AI extraction pass flagged it as a possible caregiver. There is no caregiver. See
`personas/03_verification_log.md`, entries V-11 and C-01.
